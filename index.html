Our Craftsmanship

Built for Australian Homes & Architect Specifications

At Marquis, every bathroom cabinet, vanity top, and mirror is engineered with precision joinery standards. We draw up designs directly with installers and architects to deliver bathroom furniture that stands up to daily humidity, moisture, and wear for over twenty years.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard lead time for Marquis vanities?

Standard Express items ship within 5 business days. Custom finish vanities take between 10-15 business days depending on finish choice.

What warranty is provided on Marquis joinery?

Marquis provides a 10-year cabinet warranty and a lifetime replacement warranty on all Blum soft-close door hinges and drawer runners.

How do I clean and care for my vanity top?

Use warm soapy water and a soft microfibre cloth. Avoid abrasive chemical cleaners or harsh scrubbing pads on timber or matte polytec surfaces.
